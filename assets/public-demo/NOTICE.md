## Provenance

The four pages are original project-directed vector compositions created specifically for MangaSensei. No commercial manga panel, dialogue, character or layout was copied or used as a source asset. No image-generation model produced the artwork or Japanese lettering. The SVG/code composition used OpenAI ChatGPT as an authoring assistant; all Japanese appears as explicit deterministic SVG text layers and the canonical PNGs are rendered from those source files.

The rendering fonts are not redistributed in this directory. Their exact upstream tags, file paths and integrity hashes are recorded in [`provenance/fonts.json`](provenance/fonts.json), and they remain under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
